The Story

Some moments divide life into before and after.

8:01 — Collisions of Time follows Jackson Corvin through a life shaped by love, sudden loss, memory, family, and the choices that remain after everything familiar has changed.

Moving between intimate moments and life-altering collisions, the novel asks what survives when time takes almost everything else—and whether hope can remain when certainty does not.

Rooted in emotional truth and told through fiction, 8:01 is ultimately about endurance: the people we carry with us, the memories that refuse to disappear, and the possibility of tomorrow.
