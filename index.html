8:01
Collisions of Time

What if the worst thing that ever happened to you was living?

Jackson Corvin's life is shaped by moments that refuse to remain in the past. Love, family, devastating loss, sacrifice and survival collide across a lifetime in a deeply human story about what remains when everything familiar has been taken away.

At its heart, 8:01 is not a story about surrender. It is about the stubborn persistence of hope.

The author

Dixon Davis

Dixon Davis writes character-driven fiction centered on memory, human connection, resilience and the moments that quietly alter the direction of a life.

8:01: Collisions of Time is his debut novel and the beginning of a larger body of work built around stories that refuse simple answers and characters who continue forward when surrender would be easier.
