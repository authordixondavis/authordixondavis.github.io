In Her Memory

Some people never really leave us.

My mother, believed in me when believing wasn't easy. She was part of my life, my memories, and ultimately part of the heart behind the story I wrote.

August 23 is her birthday.

So for one day, I don't want 8:01 – Collisions of Time to simply be a book for sale, instead I want it to be an experience shared.

I want it to be something given away.
